THE MORTO - ORIGINAL PREVIEW ART (v1.3)

The six runtime backgrounds use the actual generated illustration previews
shown in this conversation. They have been exported directly from those
images at their original resolution, without redrawing the art, except the requested camera-angle edit to the side yard. All exterior
views show night. The menu and supplied Morto drawings are retained.

PREVIEWS IN USE:
living.jpg - Moonlit ink living room.png
hallway.jpg - Moonlit Hallway with a Shadowed Doorway.png
kitchen.jpg - Powerless Kitchen, Sealed Door.png
fence.jpg - Midnight garden beneath a crescent moon.png
sideyard.jpg - High-mounted side-yard surveillance camera (edited from the original preview)
porch.jpg - Moonlit Porch at Midnight.png

The lossless original PNGs and an alternate living-room preview are included
in source-art/generated-backgrounds. The source ledger records image identity
and hashes. Runtime JPEG conversion uses quality 96 without chroma subsampling.
No substitute SVG environment drawings are used in this revision.
