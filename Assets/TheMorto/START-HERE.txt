THE MORTO v1.8 — DARK HOUSE

UNITY: UPDATE AN EXISTING PROJECT
Exit Play mode. Import The-Morto-Unity.unitypackage with all files selected.
Wait for compilation. For an existing game scene, use The Morto > Apply Balance To Current Scene,
then save the scene. This preserves your assigned MP3 and other scene edits.
You can also use The Morto > Create Playable Scene for a fresh scene.
Use the NEW scene it creates: an old scene can retain old tuning values.
The importer now converts all artwork to 2D textures before loading it.
Open Game view, choose 16:9, then Play. Focusing the Game view enables controls.
The Morto > Run Logic Checks tests the actual C# gameplay model.
The Unity Editor was not available in the build environment; engine rendering,
input and importing must still be checked locally. The pure C# model was
compiled and tested with Mono. Browser behavior is tested separately.

BROWSER
Open The-Morto.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The game works offline.
New Game starts Night 1. Continue keeps the latest unlocked night in this browser.

THE FLASHLIGHT DOES NOT REPEL OR STUN
The house is completely black with the flashlight off. Aim with the mouse and
hold F to reveal a limited circular area. Light only lets you see Morto.
It exposes you and draws him closer. Never hold it on to defend yourself.
An empty battery leaves hiding and moving available.

AWARENESS (v1.4 BALANCE)
Light adds 33.6 points per second: about 3 seconds to fill from empty.
With the light off, awareness naturally falls by 12 points per second.
Hiding lowers it by 35 points per second; arriving in another room removes
50 points as well. A full bar can cause one extra approach move, but no longer
cuts your close-encounter reaction window. That bonus only rearms once the
bar has cooled below 60%, preventing repeated penalties from tiny changes.

HOOFSTEPS
Hooves are audible ONLY when Morto moves INSIDE the house. Camera/exterior
movement is silent. Indoor hooves have the same volume and no directional pan,
so they tell you he is inside, not which room. There is no same-room text alert
or special arrival sound. Make brief flashlight checks, then escape or hide.

MOVEMENT, CAMERAS AND HIDING (v1.8)
Player rooms: Living room <-> Hallway <-> Kitchen.
The first approach still passes garden -> side path -> porch -> kitchen.
Once indoors Morto chooses adjacent rooms randomly. The hallway can lead to
kitchen or living room; the end rooms connect only to the hallway.
No direct jumps between kitchen and living room.

Completed hiding for 6–12 seconds while he is indoors sends him to a RANDOM
outdoor feed, clears awareness and resets cover. A six-second outdoor pause
plus his normal approach interval gives you time to recover before he enters.
He continues from that outdoor position toward the porch and kitchen.
Hooves remain indoor-only and stop immediately when he leaves the house.

CAMERA SPEAKER LURE
Open cameras, select a feed, then click PLAY SPEAKER or press L.
It costs 6% backup power, has a 10-second cooldown and holds his outdoor
approach for at least 5 seconds. He moves toward the selected speaker through
adjacent outdoor locations, with time spent in each view. Choose a location
behind him (especially the garden) to draw him away from the house.
The lure works only outdoors; it cannot rescue you after he enters.
It keeps working if you lower the monitor after triggering it.
The signal is an original electronic double-beep, not a hoof sound.

CHANGE SETTINGS YOURSELF
Existing Unity scene: select the Morto root > Morto Controller > Tuning.
Camera Drain = 1.8 (percent per second while monitor is open).
Lure Cost = 6. Lure Cooldown = 10 seconds. Lure Hold = 5 seconds.
Changing Camera Drain to 1.5 would be a smaller increase; 2.0 is more demanding.
Script defaults live in Assets/TheMorto/Runtime/NightGame.cs, class NightTuning.
Existing scenes retain Inspector values even when script defaults change.
The Morto > Apply Balance To Current Scene applies the new defaults and
preserves your assigned MP3; save the scene afterward.
Browser equivalent: Browser/model.js, monitor drain dt*1.8, lure() constants.

DIFFICULTY
Survive until 6 AM. Nights last 150 / 165 / 180 seconds.
A roll from 1 to 4 succeeds if it is <= the night number (1, 2 or 3).
Opportunities are every 4.5 / 3.8 / 3.2 seconds. He passes every exterior camera before entering the kitchen.
Inside he chooses randomly between adjacent rooms.
The close-encounter window is 3.2 / 2.8 / 2.4 seconds, shortened with no battery.
Repeated flashing is not a valid defence. Hiding forever in one room fails too.

JUMPSCARE AND YOUR MP3
The user-selected tilted widescreen skull image fills the window without
stretching; the foreground makes a quick controlled lunge. The duration follows the chosen sound clip.

Unity:
1. Drag your MP3 into Assets/TheMorto/Audio (give it a distinct name).
2. Select "The Morto - Select to tune gameplay and replace sounds" in Hierarchy.
3. On Morto Controller, drag the imported AudioClip into the Scare field.
4. Save the scene. Test a loss and adjust Volume.
If you create another new scene afterward, assign your sound again there.

Browser:
Settings > Choose jumpscare sound accepts your MP3 or WAV for the current
session. Reloading returns to the included original temporary effect.
The supplied download still uses the original temporary effect; your MP3 was
not attached. To permanently embed another sound, replace audio/scare.wav
with a WAV export and run python3 tools/build.py.

CONTROLS
Aim mouse + hold F: flashlight. A/D or Left/Right: move. H: hide/leave cover.
Space: cameras. 1–3: camera feed. P/Escape: pause. R: retry. Mouse controls are
also available. Unity supports M to mute. Losing focus pauses the game.

ART AND MENU
The previously shown illustrated room art is retained. The side-yard artwork
has been edited to show a high, wall-mounted security-camera viewpoint.
The original menu portrait is retained. The tilted widescreen image is now
the jumpscare, as requested. Six full-height

in reserved space before the next menu item. All original/edit PNGs and the
