THE MORTO v1.0

The initial outdoor route still passes every camera before the kitchen.
Indoor movement is now random between adjacent rooms. After hiding, Morto
returns to a random outdoor location and waits six seconds plus his approach
interval, allowing the player's cover recovery to finish.

Monitor backup drains 1.8% per second, up from 1.2%.
A camera speaker lure costs 6% backup power and has a 10-second cooldown.
It attracts Morto toward the selected outdoor feed through adjacent locations
and delays his approach for at least five seconds. It has no effect indoors.
The speaker tone is synthesized in MortoController.cs / Browser/game.js.

Indoor-only hoof audio, dark rooms, observational flashlight, awareness
cooldown, full-height location art, tilted jumpscare and menu stars remain.
Unity defaults can be edited in NightTuning or the scene's Tuning Inspector.
Apply Balance To Current Scene also sets the new camera/lure defaults.

v1.8: Speaker cooldown stays at 10 seconds. Attention gain increased 18 to 24 per second. Optional empty Menu Music AudioClip on MortoController loops only in Menu phase, uses master volume/mute, stops on starting a night. Browser Settings also accepts session-only menu music.

Credits button and credits overlay removed from the game menu. Asset attribution remains in ASSET-CREDITS.txt.

v1.8: Attention gain increased 40% to 33.6/second. Speaker has a 70% success chance per valid activation; failure still plays sound and consumes 6% backup with a 10-second cooldown, without moving or delaying Morto. How to Survive shortened.
